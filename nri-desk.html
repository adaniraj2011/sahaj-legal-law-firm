Legal Services for Non-Resident Indians

At Sahaj Legal Law Firm, we understand the unique legal challenges faced by Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) when dealing with matters in India.

Being away from your homeland often makes it difficult to manage property, inheritance, or legal disputes. Our dedicated NRI Desk is designed to bridge this gap and provide you with trusted, transparent, and result-oriented legal services in India.

Global Reach – Local Expertise

For the last five years, our team has been actively visiting USA, UK, Canada, Africa, Australia, and New Zealand to provide legal aid and consultations to NRIs. These visits have helped us successfully resolve numerous property and family law matters, making us one of the most trusted Indian law firms for NRIs across the globe.

Exclusive Legal Packages for NRIs

For NRIs who have been living abroad for years, we offer exclusive legal packages so that you don’t have to travel to India for your legal matters. Our expert lawyers can even visit you abroad and complete your work efficiently, giving you complete peace of mind.
